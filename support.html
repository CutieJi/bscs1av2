Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Currently, password resets are handled by administrators. Please contact the MIS Office.

Can I borrow multiple items?

Yes, as long as the items are available and you provide the room and return time for each.

What if I return an item late?

Late returns are flagged in the system. Please return items by the specified time to avoid penalties.

My department is not UCC MIS. Can I still use this?

This system is specifically integrated for UCC MIS equipment. Contact your department for its own management tools.

Common Troubleshooting

  • Camera permissions: If the QR scanner will not start, check your browser site settings and make sure camera access is allowed.
  • Network requirements: QR scanning and real-time updates require an active internet connection and should be served over HTTPS.
  • Login issues: Ensure caps lock is off and you are using the correct Student ID format.

Report an Issue

Found a bug or want to send a suggestion? Use this quick process.

  1. Take a screenshot of the error.
  2. Note what action caused the issue.
  3. Send the details to roshingel.dev@gmail.com.
