Student Guide

Use these steps to access your account and manage borrowed equipment.

  1. Login

    Access your dashboard using your student email or ID.

  2. Account Status

    If your account is pending, wait for an administrator to verify and approve your registration.

  3. Find Equipment

    Use the Browse Equipment section to search for available tools. You can filter by category or status.

  4. Borrowing and returning

    Click Borrow on an available item. Enter the Room Number and expected Return Time. The item will appear in your My Borrowed list after submission.

    To return an item, find it in My Borrowed, click Return, and select its condition. Items in good condition return to available status; damaged items are flagged for maintenance.

  5. QR Convenience

    Use the Scan feature to borrow or return equipment by scanning its physical QR code.

Admin Guide

Administrators can manage equipment, review user registrations, and prepare reports from the dashboard.

  1. System Overview

    The main dashboard provides live counts of total, borrowed, and maintenance equipment.

  2. Equipment Management

    Add creates equipment entries with unique IDs. Edit updates names, IDs, or status. QR generates and downloads codes for physical tagging.

  3. User Approvals

    Go to Users → Pending to review registrations. Approve legitimate students or reject invalid requests.

  4. Data Export

    Use Logs to filter borrowing history and export Excel/CSV reports.

Quick Tips & Best Practices

  • Equipment care: Verify cables and accessories are included before returning an item.
  • Reporting: Mark damaged items as Not Good when returning them, and leave a note if prompted.
  • Scanner setup: Use HTTPS for the QR scanner to function on mobile devices.
  • Maintenance: Administrators should regularly check the maintenance queue.
